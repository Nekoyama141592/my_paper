# 吹き出し背景の生成記録

作成日：2026-11-18

使用方法：内蔵 image_gen（CLI不使用）。文字はPowerPoint上の編集可能なテキスト。背景は2・15ページで使用。マスコットと実験刺激は既存画像のまま保持。

保存先：`question-background.png`

## 最終プロンプト

```text
Use case: productivity-visual
Asset type: reusable background for a Japanese academic presentation, exact wide 16:9 canvas.
Primary request: Create a restrained POP academic slide background. Pure white #FFFFFF background, one large flat rounded speech bubble with pale cyan #DDF4FA fill and deep navy #08245C thin outline. The bubble occupies x=4.5% to 95.5%, y=27.2% to 52.1% of the canvas, with a small downward tail near x=15%. All other areas are pure white and empty. This is for a large research question overlay.
Style: clean precise flat graphic, generous whitespace, rounded corners. No gradients, shadows, texture or three-dimensional effects.
Constraints: no text, no characters, no mascots, no icons, no logos, no numbering, no watermark. Keep all typography areas blank. Landscape 16:9.
```
